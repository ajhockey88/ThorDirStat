🗺️ The first release of ThorDirStat: WinDirStat for the AYN Thor. See what's filling your internal storage and SD card at a glance.

## ✨ What's in it
- 🗺️ **Cushion treemap** on the top screen: every file is a rectangle sized by its bytes, coloured by file type and grouped by folder, shaded like WinDirStat
- 📁 **Folder tree** on the bottom screen, biggest first, with a share bar, size, item count and last change for each item
- 🎨 **File types** list: every extension with its colour, a description, its bytes, share and file count. Tap one to outline all its files in the treemap
- 💽 **Drives:** scan internal storage, the SD card, a USB drive, or all of them at once
- 🔍 **Zoom** the treemap into any folder and back out
- ☰ **Actions** (press and hold): open, share, properties, copy path, rescan a folder, and delete with a confirmation
- `<Free space>` and `<Unknown>` show free space and the space used by apps and Android itself
- 📂 **Android/data and Android/obb** are measured too when Shizuku is allowed
- 🎮 **Gamepad controls:** D-pad, A / B / X / Y and L1 / R1 work from either screen
- 🖥️ **One-screen mode** puts the lists and treemap side by side, and **Swap screens** flips them
- ⚙️ **Settings** for treemap shading, scanning options and per-action haptics
- 🔄 **Check for updates** in Settings installs future releases straight from GitHub

## 📲 Install
1. Download `ThorDirStat.apk` below, copy it to the Thor and open it to install.
2. Open ThorDirStat, tap **Allow access**, and turn on **Allow access to manage all files**.
3. Optional: allow ThorDirStat in Shizuku to measure `Android/data` and `Android/obb`.
